Our Workflow

How We Handle Your Device

A transparent and secure 6-step repair lifecycle ensuring quality calibration and device integrity.

Step 01

Device Submission

Drop off your device at our service center. We record details, serial numbers, and generate a secure Repair ticket ID.

Step 02

Technical Diagnosis

Senior engineers test circuits, diagnose chip failure components, and estimate parts/repair service requirements.

Step 03

Customer Approval

We share a precise diagnostic report and estimate cost. Repair begins only after your explicit confirmation.

Step 04

Repair Process

Certified technicans perform hardware replacements, soldering, or firmware flashes in our clean workspace environment.

Step 05

Testing & Quality Check

Every device goes through diagnostic stress testing, software tests, and heating dissipation checks before collection release.

Step 06

Ready for Collection

Collect your device with a detailed invoice and service warranty badge. Immediate support is available via WhatsApp query.

Sakthi Systems & Services
